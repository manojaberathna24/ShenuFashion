3. Step-by-Step Video Demonstration Script

3.1 Part 1: Storefront UI & 150-Item Product Catalogue

Presenter: Shenu Weerakoon (ITBIN-2211-0126) • Time: 0:00 – 2:30

“Hello everyone, we are Group 14. Today, we are proud to demonstrate our completed E-Commerce platform for Shenu Fashion Store, a modern, luxury multi-category online apparel platform built for Sri Lankan fashion shoppers.”
On-Screen Actions:
  1. Landing Page (0:00 - 1:10): Open index.html. Highlight the responsive navigation, luxury Champagne Gold accents, and frosted glass floating badges (Fast Delivery and Secure Payment).
  2. Theme Switcher (1:10 - 1:40): Click the Sun/Moon icon in the navbar. Demonstrate seamless transition from Luxury Dark Mode to Clean Light Mode across cards, hero banners, and text.
  3. Product Catalogue (1:40 - 2:30): Click "Products" in the navbar to open pages/products.html.
    • Showcase the 150 real fashion apparel items structured across 6 categories: Dresses, Women, Men, Bags, Shoes, and Accessories.
    • Click category filter tabs (e.g. Dresses, Men).
    • Demonstrate sorting dropdown (Price: Low to High).
    • Type "Silk" into search box to demonstrate instant client-side filtering without page refresh.
“Now, I hand over to Sanjana to demonstrate our Product Details and Shopping Cart systems.”

3.2 Part 2: Product Details, Variants & Shopping Cart

Presenter: Sanjana Kiriwalla (ITBIN-2211-0128) • Time: 2:30 – 4:45

“Thank you, Shenu. I will now demonstrate our interactive Product Details and Shopping Cart components.”
On-Screen Actions:
  1. Product Detail Interaction (2:30 - 3:30): Click on any product card (e.g. Floral Silk Chiffon Midi Dress).
    • Show high-resolution photo gallery, price breakdown, and description.
    • Select variant sizes (S, M, L, XL) and color swatches.
    • Adjust the quantity selector (+ / -).
    • Scroll down to show the Customer Ratings & Verified Reviews section with star score distributions.
  2. Shopping Cart Operations (3:30 - 4:45):
    • Click "Add to Cart". Show the animated cart badge incrementing to 1 and the green confirmation toast.
    • Open pages/cart.html via the navbar.
    • Demonstrate quantity modification: Change quantity from 1 to 2; show subtotal immediately updating.
    • Point out the Free Shipping Threshold: Standard shipping is Rs. 350, but automatically applies FREE shipping on orders over Rs. 10,000.
“Now, our team leader Manoj will demonstrate our Checkout flow, Multi-Payment Gateways, and our AI Stylist Chatbot.”

3.3 Part 3: Checkout, Payment Gateways & AI Stylist Chatbot

Presenter: Manoj Aberathna (ITBIN-2211-0135) • Time: 4:45 – 7:30

“Thank you, Sanjana. I will demonstrate our Checkout process, Payment options, and our cutting-edge Shenu AI Stylist Chatbot.”
On-Screen Actions:
  1. Checkout Flow & Multi-Payment (4:45 - 5:30): Click "Proceed to Checkout" (pages/checkout.html).
    • Fill in delivery address. Point out form validation.
    • Highlight payment options: Credit/Debit Card (PayHere), Cash on Delivery (COD), and Koko Pay (3 Installments).
    • Apply Promo Code: Enter `SHENU10` — show 10% discount immediately applied!
    • Click "Place Order" — Order confirmation appears with Order ID ORD-684317 and a direct "Print Invoice" button.
  2. Shenu AI Stylist Chatbot (The Showstopper) (5:30 - 7:30):
    • Click the floating circular Cyberpunk AI Character Avatar on bottom-right. Point out the clean UI without predefined buttons.
    • Test 1 – Budget Query: Type what items have under 4500 lkr and click Send. Show real product cards returned under Rs. 4,500 with photos, prices, "Add to Cart", and "View Details". Click a card to show it loading the detail page with the ⚡ Buy Now button!
    • Test 2 – Smart Curated Gift Pack: Type mata denna 12000 kt gift pack ekak. The bot asks "Who is this gift pack for?" Click [👩 For Her]. The bot curates a 3-piece luxury bundle (Dress + Bag + Jewelry) within budget. Click "Add Entire 3-Piece Gift Pack to Cart"!
    • Test 3 – Order Tracking & Context: Type Track my order 4355 — shows live status for Kamal Gunaratne (#ORD-004355). Then type follow-up: me order eke recipient kauda? — the bot answers specifically with the recipient name!
“Now, Bandara will demonstrate our Courier Tracking timeline and the Backend Admin Operations Dashboard.”

Presenter: L. J. M. C. C. Bandara (ITBIN-2211-0151) • Time: 7:30 – 10:00

“Thank you, Manoj. I will conclude our demonstration with our Live Courier Tracking system and the Admin Operations Dashboard.”

    • Navigate to Admin Products (pages/admin/products.html): Demonstrate real-time inventory management and stock level indicators.
  3. Conclusion & Wrap-Up (9:45 - 10:00):
    “In conclusion, our Group 14 platform fully implements all required E-Commerce pillars: Product Catalogue, Shopping Cart, Secure Checkout, AI Customer Support, and an Admin Operations system. The platform is 100% operational and deployed live on GitHub Pages. Thank you!”
